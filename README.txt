STAT 201 Study Lab
==================

How to open
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).

Notes
- It is one self-contained file. Your progress is saved in your browser on this device.
- An internet connection is only used for the fonts. Without it, the page still works with default fonts.
- Use the Theme button at the top right to switch between Auto, Light and Dark.
- Start at the Overview tab, then follow the lessons in order. Each lesson has sections A to K; use the Show switch to move from Beginner to Academic application.
- The Vocabulary tab lists every academic term with its plain-English meaning.
